# ♻️ Kabadiwala Connect

> **AI-Powered E-Waste Collection, Valuation & Recycler Connection Platform**

Kabadiwala Connect is a digital platform designed to make the e-waste recycling process more transparent, efficient, and accessible. The platform helps users identify e-waste materials, estimate their market value, connect with authorized recyclers, and track the handover process.

## 🎯 Problem

India generates a large amount of electronic waste every year, but many people are unaware of:

- The actual value of their e-waste
- Authorized recycling options
- Proper e-waste disposal methods
- Current market rates
- Important recoverable materials and critical minerals

Kabadiwala Connect aims to solve this problem through technology, AI-based material identification, transparent pricing, and digital recycler connectivity.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 AI Material Identification
Identify e-waste categories using image-based analysis and predefined material categories.

### 💰 Smart Valuation
Estimate the approximate value of e-waste based on:

- Material category
- Weight
- Condition
- Current estimated market rate

### ⚖️ Transparent Price Comparison
Compare the estimated formal recycling value with other selling options.

### ♻️ Recycler Matching
Connect users with suitable and authorized recycling partners.

### 📱 Digital Handover Process
Generate a digital handover workflow for secure material transfer.

### 📊 User Dashboard
Track:

- Material valuation
- Estimated earnings
- Recycler information
- Handover status
- Recycling journey

### 🌐 Multilingual Interface
The platform supports multiple language options, including:

- English
- Hindi
- Punjabi

### 🎙️ Voice Assistance
Voice-enabled interaction helps make the platform easier to use.

### 🛡️ Safety & Awareness
Provides guidance for responsible and safer e-waste disposal.
